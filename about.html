About Nexum

Built by trainers who still teach

We started as a single classroom in Upper Hill with one mission — make practical tech, business and language skills reachable for anyone willing to show up and put in the work.

Our story

From one classroom to a full campus

Nexum Training Center opened its doors in Upper Hill, Nairobi, with a handful of desktop computers and a conviction that skills training should be judged by what students can build, not just what they can recite.

Since then we've expanded from computer packages into web development, accounting, graphics and language instruction — always keeping class sizes small enough that no student is just a name on a register.

What drives us

Mission, vision and values

Mission

To equip students and working professionals with practical ICT, business and language skills they can use from day one.

Vision

To be Nairobi's most trusted training center for career-ready technology and business skills.

Core values

Practicality, patience with beginners, honesty about job-market realities, and pride in student outcomes.
