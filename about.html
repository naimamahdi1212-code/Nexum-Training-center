Our Story

From one computer lab to a full training center

Nexum Training Center began as a small computer lab helping local students pass their ICT proficiency exams. As demand grew, so did our curriculum — expanding into web development, accounting software, graphic design and language classes for professionals who needed practical, job-ready skills fast.

Today, Nexum is a multi-disciplinary training center known for small class sizes, trainers who work in the industry they teach, and a hands-on approach that treats every learner as a future professional, not just a student.
